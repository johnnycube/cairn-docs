# Training load

Cairn computes the classic training-load model from your full activity history:

- **CTL** (Chronic Training Load) — your *fitness*, a long exponentially-weighted
  average of daily load.
- **ATL** (Acute Training Load) — your *fatigue*, a short average.
- **TSB** (Training Stress Balance = CTL − ATL) — your *form*.

These are charted under **Analysis**. The chart series colors are fixed and
independent of the brand accent, so fitness/fatigue/form always read the same.

Load is recomputed as a follow-up on every import, so adding or correcting a
source updates your curves.
